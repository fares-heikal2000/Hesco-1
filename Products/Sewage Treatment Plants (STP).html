Sewage Treatment Plants (STP)

Introduction

HESCO Water Integrated Systems provides reliable and customized solutions for the treatment of domestic sewage and wastewater generated from residential, commercial, hospitality, and institutional facilities.

Our STP solutions are designed to achieve the required treated-water quality in compliance with applicable environmental regulations, while providing efficient operation, low maintenance requirements, and flexibility for water reuse.

Applications

  • Hotels & Resorts
  • Residential Compounds
  • Commercial Buildings & Malls
  • Hospitals & Healthcare Facilities
  • Schools & Universities
  • Administrative & Office Buildings
  • Camps & Labor Accommodation
  • Public & Institutional Facilities

Main Treatment Components

  • Screening & Grit Removal
  • Equalization Tank
  • Biological Treatment
  • Aeration System
  • Secondary Clarification
  • MBBR / MBR Systems
  • Tertiary Treatment & Filtration
  • Disinfection – Chlorination / UV
  • Sludge Handling & Dewatering
  • Treated Water Storage & Reuse Systems
  • Chemical Dosing Systems
  • Instrumentation, PLC & SCADA

Typical Treatment Process

Preliminary Treatment → Equalization → Biological Treatment → Clarification → Tertiary Treatment → Disinfection → Treated Water Reuse / Disposal
