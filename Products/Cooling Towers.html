Cooling Towers

A Cooling Tower is a heat rejection system designed to remove heat from industrial process water by transferring heat to atmospheric air through evaporation and direct contact between water and air.

Cooling towers are widely used in industrial plants, HVAC systems, power generation, and manufacturing processes.

At HESCO Water Integrated Systems, we design, manufacture, and supply cooling tower solutions using different international and local technologies.

Cooling Tower Operation Depends On

  1. Hot water enters the tower distribution system.
  2. Water flows through the fill material.
  3. Fans move air through the tower.
  4. Heat transfers from water to air through evaporation.
  5. Cooled water returns back to the process.

Cooling Tower Types

  • Open Circuit Cooling Towers
  • Closed Circuit Cooling Towers
  • Counter Flow Cooling Towers
  • Cross Flow Cooling Towers
  • FRP Cooling Towers
  • Galvanized Steel Cooling Towers
