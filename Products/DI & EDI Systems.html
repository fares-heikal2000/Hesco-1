DI & EDI Systems

Demineralization & EDI Systems

DI (Deionization) and EDI (Electrodeionization) systems are advanced purification technologies used to remove dissolved ions and produce high-purity water.

DI

  • Water passes through cation and anion exchange resins.
  • Dissolved ions are removed.

EDI

  • Uses electrical current and membranes to continuously remove ions without chemical regeneration.
