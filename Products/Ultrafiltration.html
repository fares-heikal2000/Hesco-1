Ultrafiltration (UF) Systems

Introduction

At HESCO Water Integrated Systems, we provide advanced Ultrafiltration (UF) systems designed to deliver high-quality treated water through reliable membrane filtration technology.

UF technology uses high-performance membranes with microscopic pores to remove suspended solids, turbidity, colloidal particles, bacteria, and other microorganisms, ensuring consistent water quality and reliable protection for downstream treatment processes, especially Reverse Osmosis (RO) systems.

UF System Applications

Our Ultrafiltration solutions are designed for a wide range of industrial and commercial applications, including:

  • Pretreatment for Reverse Osmosis (RO) Systems.
  • Surface Water Treatment.
  • Groundwater Treatment.
  • Industrial Process Water.
  • Wastewater Treatment & Reuse.
  • Tertiary Treatment.
  • Drinking Water Pretreatment.

UF System Technologies & Configurations

HESCO offers different UF membrane configurations and system designs to meet various water treatment requirements, including:

  • Outside-In and Inside-Out Membrane Configurations.
  • Pressurized UF Systems.
  • Skid-Mounted UF Units.
  • Automatic Backwashing Systems.
  • Chemical Enhanced Backwash (CEB) Systems.
  • Clean-in-Place (CIP) Systems.
  • Customized Modular UF Systems.
