Reverse Osmosis (RO) Systems

Reverse Osmosis (RO) is one of the most advanced water purification technologies. It removes dissolved salts, minerals, bacteria, viruses, and contaminants through semi-permeable membranes, producing high-quality water suitable for industrial and drinking applications.

Deionization - DI & Electrodeionization - EDI

Deionization (DI) Systems & Electrodeionization (EDI) Systems

DI systems remove dissolved ions using cation and anion exchange resins to produce high-purity water.
EDI combines ion exchange resins with electrical current to continuously produce ultrapure water without chemical regeneration.

  • Pharmaceutical plants
