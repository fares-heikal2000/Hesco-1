Multimedia Filtration Systems

Introduction

At HESCO Water Integrated Systems, we design, manufacture, and supply advanced Multimedia Filtration Systems engineered to remove suspended solids, turbidity, and particulate contaminants from water.

Our filtration systems utilize carefully selected layers of filtration media with different particle sizes and densities to achieve efficient filtration, improve water quality, and protect downstream treatment equipment and processes.

Multimedia Filtration Applications

Our filtration solutions are suitable for a wide range of industrial and commercial applications, including:

  • Reverse Osmosis (RO) Pretreatment.
  • Industrial Process Water Treatment.
  • Groundwater & Surface Water Filtration.
  • Cooling Tower Makeup Water Treatment.
  • Boiler Feed Water Pretreatment.
  • Wastewater Treatment & Reuse.
  • General Industrial & Commercial Water Filtration.

Filtration Media & Configurations

HESCO offers different filtration configurations depending on raw water characteristics and required treatment quality, including:

  • Sand & Gravel Filters.
  • Multimedia Filters (Sand, Anthracite & Gravel).
  • Activated Carbon Filters (GAC).
  • Iron & Manganese Removal Filters.
  • Specialized Media Filters.
  • Dual Media & Multi-Layer Filtration Systems.
  • Manual & Automatic Filtration Systems.

Complete System Solutions

We provide complete engineering solutions covering all stages of the project, from raw water analysis and filtration design to manufacturing, installation, and commissioning:

Raw Water Analysis → Media Selection → Filtration System → Filtered Water

Each system is designed according to the required flow rate, raw water quality, filtration efficiency, and operating conditions. Automatic backwashing systems can be incorporated to maintain filtration performance and extend media service life.
