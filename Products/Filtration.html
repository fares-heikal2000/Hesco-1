Filtration Systems

Introduction

HESCO Water Integrated Systems provides advanced Ultrafiltration (UF) systems for the removal of suspended solids, colloids, bacteria, and other high-molecular-weight contaminants from water.

UF technology uses membrane separation to produce consistent and high-quality filtered water, making it an effective solution for water pretreatment and water reuse applications.

Our UF systems can be designed as standalone treatment units or integrated with other treatment technologies such as Reverse Osmosis (RO), Multimedia Filtration, Activated Carbon Filtration, and other advanced treatment processes.

Applications

  • RO Pretreatment
  • Surface Water Treatment
  • Groundwater Treatment
  • Drinking Water Pretreatment
  • Wastewater Tertiary Treatment
  • Water Reuse
  • Industrial Process Water
  • Cooling Tower Makeup Water
  • Municipal Water Treatment
  • Pretreatment for High-Purity Water Systems

What Does Ultrafiltration Remove?

UF membranes provide an effective barrier against:

  • Suspended Solids (TSS)
  • Colloidal Particles
  • Turbidity
  • Bacteria
  • Microorganisms
  • High-Molecular-Weight Organic Matter
  • Fine Particles

UF is primarily a physical separation process and does not normally remove dissolved salts and low-molecular-weight dissolved substances. For applications requiring desalination, UF can be used as an effective pretreatment stage before Reverse Osmosis.

How Does Ultrafiltration Work?

During operation, feed water passes through UF membranes under pressure. Water and smaller dissolved components pass through the membrane as permeate, while suspended particles, colloids, and microorganisms are retained and removed through the concentrate stream.

Feed Water → UF Membranes → Permeate + Concentrate

Depending on the membrane configuration and application, the system can operate in different filtration modes and may include periodic backwashing and chemical cleaning to maintain membrane performance.

UF System Operation

A typical UF system may include several operating stages:

Filtration → Backwash → Air Scouring, where applicable → Rinse → Return to Service

Periodic Chemical Enhanced Backwash (CEB) and Clean-In-Place (CIP) procedures can also be incorporated according to membrane manufacturer requirements and feed-water characteristics.

UF + RO Integration

UF + RO Integration

For applications requiring desalination, UF can be installed as an advanced pretreatment stage before RO. By reducing suspended solids, colloids, and turbidity in the RO feed water, UF can help provide more stable RO operation and reduce the risk of particulate and colloidal fouling.

Raw Water → Pretreatment → UF → Cartridge Filter → RO → Product Water
