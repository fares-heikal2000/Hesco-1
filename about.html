How it all started ?

HESCO...
for Water Integrated Systems is an Egyptian engineering company specializing in the design, manufacturing, supply, installation, operation, and maintenance of water treatment systems and cooling towers. Established in 2026, the company was built upon the extensive experience of a highly qualified engineering and technical team that has successfully delivered projects with leading companies across the water treatment and industrial sectors.

Within a short period, HESCO has successfully become a partner in the success of several prestigious organizations, including Egyptian Steel, Misr Beni Suef Cement, Alumil Egypt, Domty, EIPICO Pharmaceuticals, UNI-CHARM Egypt, and The St. Regis New Capital, reflecting our commitment to delivering reliable, high-quality engineering solutions and building long-term customer relationships.

At HESCO, we believe that technical excellence, innovation, and customer satisfaction are the foundation of every successful project. Our mission is to provide reliable, energy-efficient, and cost-effective solutions that meet the highest engineering standards while addressing the unique requirements of each client. Our expertise covers a comprehensive range of water treatment technologies, including Reverse Osmosis (RO), Ultrafiltration (UF), Media Filtration Systems, Water Softeners, Deionization (DI), Electrodeionization (EDI), Drinking Water Treatment Plants, Industrial Wastewater Treatment Plants, and Sewage Treatment Plants. In addition to water treatment solutions, HESCO specializes in the design and manufacturing of high-performance cooling towers. Our product range includes Open Circuit Crossflow and Counterflow Cooling Towers manufactured in Fiberglass (FRP), Hot-Dip Galvanized Steel, and Stainless Steel, as well as Closed Circuit Cooling Towers designed for industrial and commercial applications. We also manufacture and supply all major cooling tower components, including fills, drift eliminators, louvers, nozzles, fans, and mechanical accessories. We are committed to delivering sustainable engineering solutions, dependable aftersales support, and exceptional value that enable our clients to improve operational efficiency, reduce operating costs, and achieve long-term reliability.
