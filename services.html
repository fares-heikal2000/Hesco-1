WATER TREATMENT

Wastewater Treatment Plants

Wastewater Treatment is an integrated process designed to remove contaminants, organic matter, suspended solids, and harmful substances from industrial and domestic wastewater to produce treated water suitable for safe discharge, reuse, or recycling.

HESCO provides customized wastewater treatment solutions for different industrial sectors including food & beverage, pharmaceutical, chemical, textile, and manufacturing industries.

At HESCO Water Integrated Systems, we provide complete wastewater treatment solutions using a wide range of international and local brands.

Typical Wastewater Treatment Process

01
Screening

Removal of large suspended materials and solid particles.

02
Equalization Tank

Balancing flow and wastewater characteristics before treatment.

03
Biological Treatment

Using microorganisms to reduce organic load (COD & BOD).

04
Clarification

Separation of biological sludge from treated water.

05
Filtration

Removal of remaining suspended particles.

06
Disinfection

Elimination of microorganisms before reuse or discharge.

MAINTENANCE SERVICES

Chemical Cleaning & Descaling

Chemical Cleaning Services

Chemical cleaning is a controlled process used to remove accumulated scale, deposits, biological growth, corrosion products, oils, and other contaminants from water treatment and industrial equipment.

It Is Commonly Applied To

  • RO Membranes
  • Heat Exchangers
  • Cooling Towers
  • Chillers
  • Boilers
  • Filtration Systems
  • Industrial Piping

The Cleaning Process Generally Includes

01
System Inspection

Assessment of the equipment condition and identification of the type of fouling.

02
Chemical Selection

Selection of the appropriate cleaning chemical based on the contaminant and equipment material.

03
Preparation & Isolation

The equipment is isolated and connected to the chemical cleaning system.

04
Chemical Circulation

The cleaning solution is circulated through the equipment under controlled conditions.

05
Flushing

The system is thoroughly flushed with clean water to remove chemical residues.

06
Final Inspection & Testing

System performance is checked before returning the equipment to normal operation.
